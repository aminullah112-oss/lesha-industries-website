About Lesha Industries

A service-focused industrial solutions company based in Jeddah, supporting oil & gas, petrochemical, manufacturing, marine and construction industries.

Who We Are

A Service-Focused Industrial Solutions Company

Lesha Industries is a service-focused industrial solutions company dedicated to supporting the oil & gas, petrochemical, manufacturing, marine, and construction industries. We specialize in providing reliable supply, technical support, and end-to-end service solutions for a wide range of industrial requirements.

With a skilled team of experienced sales engineers and technical professionals, we are committed to delivering efficient, high-quality, and cost-effective solutions tailored to our customers’ operational needs. Our expertise covers steel products, industrial equipment, electrical and instrumentation materials, tools, valves, fittings, building materials, and Personal Protective Equipment (PPE).

  • Based in As Samir District, Jeddah
  • Supply, fabrication, welding & machinery servicing under one roof
  • Custom engineering on request
