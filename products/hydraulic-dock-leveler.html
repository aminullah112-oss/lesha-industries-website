Hydraulic Dock Leveler

A hydraulically operated dock leveler engineered to bridge the gap between a warehouse loading dock and a trailer bed, giving forklifts and pallet jacks a safe, stable crossing for continuous loading and unloading. Built to EN 1398:2009 design standard in ASTM A36 steel.

  • Hydraulic structure cylinder with double-acting hydraulic lip cylinder
  • Bridges the dock-to-trailer gap for safe material handling access
  • Heavy-duty rubber & steel fenders and a safety lock bar for maintenance
  • Customizable platform size and load capacity from 6/8/10 up to 40 tons

Key Specification — Model LI-DL-6T

Capacity6 / 8 / 10 up to 40T (customizable)
Platform size2000 x 3000 mm (customizable)
Powered byHydraulic – 0.75 Kw electric motor, 220/380V 3-phase
Operation / Max. pressure80 bar / 120 bar
Vertical working range+600 mm above deck / -300 mm below deck

Weight (approx.)1300 kg (varies with capacity & size)
Platform materialSteel non-slip checker plate
Design standardEN 1398:2009, ASTM A36 material grade
