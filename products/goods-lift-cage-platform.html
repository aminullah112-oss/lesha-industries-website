Goods Lift Cage & Platform

A hydraulic cargo mast lift that raises a fenced platform along a fixed mast for safe vertical transfer of goods between warehouse floors and mezzanines, powered by an Italian-made hydraulic power pack.

  • 2 ton rated capacity on a 1500 x 1500 mm non-slip steel platform
  • 4000 mm lift height, 6500 mm overall mast height
  • Hose-burst security valve blocks the platform in case of a hose failure
  • Safety limit switches in case a lift wire is cut, plus safety instruction stickers
  • Custom capacities and sizes available on request
