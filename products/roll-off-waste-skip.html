Roll-Off Waste Skip

Conic-shaped steel skip bins, built for convenient shipping, stacking and storage, and loaded by hydraulic lifting mechanism, side hooks or winch. Available in 6, 12, 20 and 30 cubic yard capacities — download the datasheet for the size you need.

  • 3 mm sheet steel with reinforced 3 mm walls
  • Optional top cover and drain plug
  • Compatible with hydraulic hook-lift, side-hook and winch loading systems
  • Custom colour finish available on request
